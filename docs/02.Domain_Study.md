# Domain Study

## 1. Domain Name
Healthcare

## 2. Domain Overview
Home Healthcare is a healthcare service where qualified nurses visit patients at their homes to provide medical care, treatment, and follow-up services. This is useful for elderly people, patients recovering after surgery, people with chronic diseases, and those who cannot travel to hospitals easily.

## 3. Existing System
Most home healthcare services are managed manually using phone calls, paper records, or spreadsheets. This often leads to appointment conflicts, scheduling delays, difficulty in tracking nurse visits, and poor communication between patients and nurses.

## 4. Proposed System
The proposed Home Healthcare Nurse Visiting Schedule system is a web-based application that allows patients to request home nurse visits online. The admin can manage patients, assign nurses, and schedule appointments. Nurses can view their schedules and update visit reports after completing each visit.

## 5. Users
- Admin
- Nurse
- Patient

## 6. Features
- Patient Registration
- Nurse Management
- Appointment Booking
- Nurse Visit Scheduling
- Visit Report Management
- Appointment Status Tracking

## 7. Benefits
- Easy appointment booking
- Efficient nurse scheduling
- Reduced manual work
- Better patient care
- Accurate record management
- Time-saving and reliable service
