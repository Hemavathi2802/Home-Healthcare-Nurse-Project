# Problem Statement

## 1. Title
Home Healthcare Nurse Visiting Schedule

## 2. Domain
Healthcare

## 3. Who is the user? 
- Nurse – Views assigned visits, updates visit status, and submits visit reports.
- Patient – Books home healthcare appointments and tracks nurse visits.

## 4. What problem are we solving? 
Home healthcare services are often managed manually through phone calls and paper records. This results in appointment conflicts, scheduling delays, and poor communication between patients and healthcare providers. Patients may not know the visit status, while administrators find it difficult to assign available nurses efficiently. A digital scheduling system is required to simplify appointment booking and nurse visit management.

## 5. Proposed Solution 
The application allows patients to book home healthcare appointments online. Administrators can manage patients, assign nurses based on availability, and monitor all appointments. Nurses can view their assigned schedules, update visit status, and submit visit reports. The system provides a centralized platform for efficient home healthcare management.

## 6. Core Entities / Database Tables 
- Admin
- Patient
- Nurse
- Appointment
- Visit Schedule
- Visit Report

## 7. User Roles & Permissions 
### Admin
- Manage patients
- Manage nurses
- Assign nurses
- Manage appointments
- View reports

### Nurse
- View assigned schedule
- Update visit status
- Submit visit report

### Patient
- Register and login
- Book appointments
- View appointment status
- View visit history

## 8. Success Criteria
- A patient should be able to book a home healthcare appointment in under one minute.
- An administrator should be able to assign a nurse quickly without scheduling conflicts.
- Nurses should be able to update visit status and reports efficiently.

## 9. Out of Scope
- Online payment gateway
- Video consultation
- Ambulance booking
- Pharmacy delivery
- Laboratory test booking

## 10. Chosen Track
Java (Spring Boot)
